About

Editing is how I think.

I'm Olle Rang, a documentary-style video editor based in Stockholm, Sweden, working with combat sports athletes and creators to shape raw footage into stories that land.

Cut entirely in DaVinci Resolve, I specialise in vlogs and documentary-style storytelling for combat sports, always in pursuit of the cut that feels invisible and resonates with the audience.
